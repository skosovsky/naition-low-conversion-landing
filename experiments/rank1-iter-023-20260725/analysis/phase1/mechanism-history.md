## Mechanisms that must not be repeated

The following are repetitions even when expressed with different copy, CSS or
labels:

- stronger free/zero-price, no-obligation, payment or commitment reassurance;
- another bonus/download/value exchange for contact data;
- another audience, outcome, fear, learnability, practice, curriculum or
  delivery-detail rewrite using only facts already on the page;
- generic trust language without new offer-bound, attributable evidence;
- form relocation, progress UI, auto-focus, synthetic continuation, prefill,
  submit-path or persisted-success changes;
- calendar widgets, fixed-date emphasis or nearby logistics restatements;
- Basic badges, defaults, recommendation sentences, plan labels, DOM/ARIA
  semantics, equalized cards, hidden paid CTAs or further action reduction;
- hiding, shortening, accordionizing or progressively disclosing the supporting
  page content;
- more programme numbering, icons, timeline styling or module reordering;
- another injury-card or cross-section content-order permutation.

Combining two or more closed families is also a repetition and destroys
attribution. Reverting a loser to exact C7 is control restoration, not a new
mechanism. Selector tricks, hidden/off-screen persuasion, bot branching,
synthetic events or weaker conversion semantics are metric gaming, not
experiments.

## Truly causally new high-capacity families

The local evidence supports **no currently deployable, truth-safe family with a
credible `+11`-order capacity**. The remaining high-capacity directions change
product reality rather than presentation:

| Priority | New family | Why it is causally new | Required truth artifact / gate | Current status |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Date-flexible open enrollment | Expands real temporal eligibility instead of reframing the fixed session | Owner-backed commitment that later-group applications are retained, contacted after date assignment, fulfilled as the same free full course in Moscow | Best plausible capacity (`32–43/101` planning range in prior audit), but blocked |
| 2 | Attributable offer-bound provider proof | Adds verifiable trust evidence rather than generic credibility copy | Publishable organizer/instructor/venue/participant evidence tied to this exact course plus a verification route and permission | New, population-wide, but absent and not proven to reach `41/101` |
| 3 | Enforceable enrollment/fulfillment policy | Changes actual uncertainty around response, cancellation, transfer, rescheduling or cutoff | Named operational owner and an enforceable SLA/policy reflected by real fulfillment behavior | New and potentially high-capacity, but absent |
| 4 | Immediate confirmed-seat transaction | Changes the product from contact-only application to real-time confirmed enrollment | Available-seat source of truth, confirmation/fulfillment workflow and authorization to alter the protected transaction contract | Causally new but outside current protected form/backend scope |

“Collect fewer fields” or a lower-intent lead is not an eligible shortcut: it
would alter the protected form and weaken the persisted conversion contract.
Likewise, merely claiming flexible dates, availability, an SLA, certification
or provider authority without the corresponding artifact would fabricate the
product.

is supplied. Without that, another presentation-only iteration is expected to
relabel a closed family or overfit serial simulator noise rather than provide a
credible route from `30` to `41` persisted orders.

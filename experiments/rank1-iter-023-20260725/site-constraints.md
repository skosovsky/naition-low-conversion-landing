# C23 site constraints

Source: root `STUDENTS.md`, read before implementation.

## Protected simulator contract

- keep `#registration-form` with `action="api/submit.php"`;
- keep fields `name`, `phone`, `email`;
- keep `<script src="api/visit.php">`;
- keep `.btn-register`, `.pricing-section`, `.program-module`,
  `.program-list`;
- do not change `api/**` or `sql/schema.sql`.

## Permitted C23 surface

`STUDENTS.md` permits moving elements inside a section. C23 only permutes six
complete `.injury-card` articles inside their existing `.injury-grid`.
The articles remain default-visible and the DOM order equals the visual and
accessibility order.

### Why rank 1 is different

`date-flexible-open-enrollment` changes real eligibility. A fixed
`15 August 2026, 10:00-18:00, Moscow` session can exclude otherwise interested
people. Accepting the same application for a later Moscow group can therefore
act on a real constraint, unlike repeating the date or making Basic clearer.

The conditional `32-43` range is deliberately wide because no local artifact
measures schedule-conflict prevalence. Its point `36` assumes a meaningful but
not dominant conflict subgroup; `41+` remains an upper-tail outcome, not a
promise. It is the only remaining family for which eleven-order arithmetic is
plausible at all.

Provider proof and enforceable policy are population-visible, but the local
record contains neither the facts nor a subgroup that supports an eleven-order
effect. They are ranked as future product unlocks, not current experiments.

## Hard no-go conditions

Do not run C22 if any of the following is true:

- any later-group owner predicate is missing or cannot be fulfilled;
- the same full free course or Moscow location is not guaranteed;
- future applicants will not actually be retained and contacted;
- copy implies a scheduled date, reserved seat, accepted participation,
  persisted preference, availability, response time or deadline not proven by
  the owner contract;
- implementation changes protected fields, form action, API/SQL, conversion
  semantics or simulator-facing selectors;
- a plan click, form open, download, question or generic contact is counted as
  an order;
- behavior varies by bot/session/user-agent or manufactures
  focus/input/submit/success;
- exact C7 runtime cannot be demonstrated as the control;
- a second simulator invocation would be required to replace an inconvenient
  valid result.
